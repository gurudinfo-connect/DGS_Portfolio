Awesome Chocolates — Power BI Dashboard

A sales-performance dashboard for a chocolate manufacturer, modeled from five related tables into a star schema, with a single date-range slider driving every KPI and chart on the page.

Power BI DAX Data Modeling
Power BI Report (.pbix)

Overview

A Power BI sales-performance report built for a chocolate manufacturer, covering shipments, products, salespeople, regions and time as five related tables joined into a clean star schema. A single date-range slider at the top of the report drives total amount, total boxes, total profit and profit % for whatever period is selected — from one month to a full year.

Key Features

Star-Schema Data Model

Shipments, products, people, locations and calendar tables linked into a star schema for fast, accurate aggregation.

Date-Range Slider

One slider control drives total amount, total boxes, total profit and profit % for any custom period.

Salesperson Leaderboard

Top-6 salespersons table with photos, totals and profit %, flagged green / amber / red against target.

Product & Geo Breakdown

A treemap of top products by amount, a full product profit-% table, and a donut of amount by geography.

Role & Challenges

I modeled the shipments, products, people, locations and calendar tables into a star schema in Power Query, then built the KPI cards, salesperson leaderboard and treemap in Power BI, wiring everything to a shared date-range slider so every visual updates together.
